About the Game

The mancala games are a family of two-player turn-based strategy board games played with small stones, beans, or seeds and rows of holes or pits. The objective is usually to capture all or some set of the opponent's pieces.

This implementation brings the world's oldest strategy games to the modern era. Whether you are a fan of the Western Kalah, the African Oware, or the Southeast Asian Congkak, you will find your favorite variant here.

Kalah

The most popular Western variant. Players move seeds counter-clockwise around the board, aiming to land the last seed in their "store" for an extra move or capture opponent's seeds by landing in an empty pit.

Oware

A classic West African game of calculation. The goal is to capture seeds by bringing the count in an opponent's pit to exactly two or three. It is widely considered one of the most strategic variants in the family.

Congkak

A traditional Southeast Asian variant played with a boat-shaped board. It features a unique "lap" system where players continue their turn as long as the last seed lands in a non-empty pit, creating long, flowing sequences.

Mangala

A traditional Turkish variant that introduces unique tactical elements, such as the ability to move seeds from the opponent's side and specific rules for doubling seeds to make captures.

Perfect for fans of strategy and logic games, Mancala helps you train your strategy skills while enjoying a relaxing yet challenging experience.
