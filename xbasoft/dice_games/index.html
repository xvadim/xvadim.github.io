About the Games

This collection brings together the best dice games, offering a perfect mix of luck and strategy. Whether you want a quick game of Pig or a deep tactical match in Thousand, we have it all.

Our app is designed for smooth gameplay with a clean interface. Play solo to sharpen your skills or join our online community to prove you are the dice master.

General

General (Yahtzee style) is a classic game where you roll five dice to make specific combinations and score the highest points.

Thousand

Thousand is a strategic risk-taking game. Roll dice to collect points, but be careful—if you don't score, you lose your turn's progress!

Dice Dodge

Dice Dodge is a unique tactical board game where you use dice to move your pieces and outsmart your opponent.

Pig

Pig is a simple and addictive push-your-luck game. Roll a die to accumulate points, but if you roll a 1, you lose everything for that turn!
