About the Games

This app brings together the most popular variations of the Golf card game family and the classic Scat (31). These games are perfect for fans of strategy, memory, and logic.

Whether you are playing solo against our smart AI or competing with real players online, you will enjoy the premium feel and intuitive controls designed for the best mobile experience.

6 Cards Golf

The goal of Six Cards Golf is to earn as few points as possible by combining your six cards into pairs of equal value, which cancel each other out.

4 Cards Golf

Four Cards Golf is a challenging variant where you can't see all your cards and must remember their values to make the best pairs and minimize your score.

Scat (31)

Scat, also known as 31, is a classic game where the goal is to have a hand with a total value equal to or as close to 31 as possible in a single suit.
