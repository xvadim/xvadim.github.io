About the Game

Seega is an ancient Egyptian board game, similar to checkers and Alquerque, that has been played for centuries. Unlike many other games, Seega doesn't start with pieces on the board; instead, players take turns placing their pieces in a strategic deployment phase.

Once the board is nearly full, the movement phase begins. You capture your opponent's pieces by surrounding them on two opposite sides. It's a game of positioning, traps, and tactical maneuvers.

Our version brings this historical treasure to your mobile device with beautiful graphics, smooth animations, and a global multiplayer community. Perfect for fans of strategy and history alike.
